🎧 Mr. Mic

One key — or just the headset power button — switches everything.

What it does: switches the Windows default speaker AND microphone together between your HyperX Cloud II Core Wireless and the laptop speakers + mic — including the communications device Discord uses. Shows headset battery. Has a built-in per-app mixer.

Everyday use

ActionWhat happens
Turn headset onEverything switches to HyperX (~2 s) — rising chime
Turn headset offEverything switches to laptop (~4 s) — falling chime
Ctrl + Alt + HManual toggle, anytime
Left-click tray iconManual toggle
Middle-click (mouse wheel) tray iconOpens the mixer
Right-click tray iconMenu: devices, battery, settings, exit

Tray icon colors

● Green headsetYou're on the HyperX
● Orange speakerYou're on laptop speakers + mic
● Gray speakerSome other device is default (e.g. ButtKicker)

Hover the icon to see current devices and battery %. Low-battery warning pops at 20%.

The mixer (middle-click)

  • Each output device: name, master slider, 🔊 mute button on the right.
  • Each app under it: icon + slider. Click the icon to mute/unmute that app (icon dims when muted). Hover the icon to see the app's name.
  • 🎤 rows at the bottom: your microphones' input levels — turn the laptop mic down here if a dictation app picks up room noise.
  • The ● marks the current default device. Esc or click away to close.

Changing the hotkey

Right-click → Edit settings → change "hotkey" → right-click → Reload settings. Examples:

ctrl+alt+hmodifier combo (default)
pauseone single key
v+ctwo keys pressed together
shift+ctrl+pany modifier mix

Settings quick reference (settings.json)

Tips & troubleshooting

  • Discord: set its input/output to Default in Voice settings once — then it always follows Mr. Mic.
  • Parlez/dictation going crazy on switch? Pin it to the HyperX mic in its own settings, or lower the laptop mic in the mixer.
  • Battery shows “—”: headset is off (that's also how auto-detect knows).
  • App not running? Double-click MrMic.exe (or run-mrmic.bat). It also autostarts with Windows. A second copy won't start twice — that's intentional.
  • Gaming: Mr. Mic runs at below-normal priority, ~0.05% CPU, no GPU — it cannot affect DCS/VR performance.
  • Logs: mrmic.log next to the app records every switch.
